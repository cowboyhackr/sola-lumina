Sola Lumina — Aero

Aero puts your whole portfolio on one Mac screen.

Every account, every position, and what the market did to them today — no tabs to dig through, no switching between brokerage websites. Open the window and the picture is there.

Requires macOS 14 or later.

One view, every account

Hold the same stock in a rollover IRA, a Roth, and a taxable brokerage and Aero shows you the whole position at once — then breaks it down per account when you want the detail. Cash is counted too, so the total is the real total.

See the day at a glance

A heatmap colours every holding by the day's move, so the winners and the drags are obvious before you read a single number. A plain-language recap tells you where the portfolio stands and which position led or lagged.

Measure against the market

Compare your portfolio's performance against the S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow Jones, and Russell 2000 on one chart. Choose a single day, a week, a month, a quarter, a year, or the whole history — and hover anywhere on the curve to read the exact figure.

Import what you already have

Drag in CSV exports from your brokerage, or screenshots of your holdings, and Aero reads them. Up to 100 files at once. If you already have accounts, it compares what's incoming against what's there and shows you exactly what would change — line by line — before anything is written. Nothing is committed until you accept it, and you can cancel the whole thing at any point.

Built for the Mac

A native Mac app, not a web page in a window. Sign in with Apple — no password to create, no email to hand over.

Aero shares an account with Moment, so your portfolio follows you between your Mac and your iPhone.
