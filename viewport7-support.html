View Port 7 Support

Video, podcasts and audiobooks in one place — here to help.

Frequently asked questions

How do I sign in to YouTube or Spotify?

Open the site from the start screen and sign in on its own page, as you would in Safari. View Port 7 has no account of its own, and your sign-ins stay on your device.

Where do the audiobooks come from?

LibriVox, a free catalog of public domain audiobooks read by volunteers. Search it from the LibriVox screen, play a book in View Port 7's player, and add others to Up Next. Recently Played remembers where you left off.

How do I use Picture in Picture?

While a video plays, tap the Picture in Picture button in the bottom bar. A playing video also moves into Picture in Picture when you leave the app.

Does audio keep playing in the background?

Yes. Podcasts and audiobooks keep playing with the screen locked, and you can control them from the lock screen, Control Center or your headphones.

What does the app send to Sola Lumina?

Only anonymous daily counts of which features were used, which you can turn off in Settings under Privacy, and any feedback you choose to send. See the privacy policy.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later.

Contact us

Questions, problems or suggestions — use Send Feedback in the app's Settings, or email support@solalumina.com.

It helps to include your iOS version and what you were doing when something went wrong.
