View Port 7 - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Overview

View Port 7 is a viewer for video, podcasts and audiobooks on the web. It has no account and no sign-in of its own. What you watch, listen to and search for stays on your device; the app sends Sola Lumina only anonymous feature counts and any feedback you choose to send.

Kept on Your Device

Your history, bookmarks, audiobook queue, recently played books, listening positions and settings are stored on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sites You Visit

View Port 7 opens websites such as YouTube, Spotify and LibriVox, the way a web browser does. Those sites receive what any browser would send them, and anything you do or sign in to there is governed by that site's own privacy policy. Your sign-ins to those sites are kept by the system's web view on your device and are never sent to Sola Lumina. Searches for podcasts and audiobooks go directly to the services being searched.

Anonymous Feature Counts

So we can tell which parts of the app are worth improving, View Port 7 counts how often each feature is used - for example "an audiobook was played" or "LibriVox was opened" - and sends those daily totals to our own server.

A count per feature per day is the whole of it. There is no name, no account, and no device or advertising identifier attached; the totals from every copy of the app are added together, and nothing on the receiving end can tell one person's counts from another's. The pages you visit, what you watch or listen to, and what you search for are never included.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings, under Privacy, with "Share Anonymous Usage". Counts not yet sent are discarded when you do.

Feedback

If you use Send Feedback in Settings, we receive the message you type, the type you picked, and the app's version. An email address is included only if you enter one, and is used only to reply to you.

No Third-Party Services

View Port 7 does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Nothing about your use of the app is shared with, sold to, or processed by anyone else, and the app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children's Privacy

View Port 7 collects no personal data from anyone, including children. The feature counts above identify nobody, of any age.
